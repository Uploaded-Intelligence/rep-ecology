The Ecological Nexus: the federation as living tissue. Each being is a biome-cell — organelle-groves glowing, nucleus veiled. A live negotiation is a braided filament carrying signals both ways; sustained exchange thickens it into permanent tissue, and quiet lets it thin — visibly, so one touch re-opens the channel instead of a guilt-list. The unmet ecology drifts as spore-light: potential, not absence. One pinch runs the whole navigation: cell ⟷ nexus ⟷ biosphere.
